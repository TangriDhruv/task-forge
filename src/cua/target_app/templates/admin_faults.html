Per-request fault codes (append to any URL, e.g. /member/M-10001?fault=slow):
CodeEffect
not_foundForces a "record not found" outcome on member lookups.
validation_errorForces the sub-account or transfer form to fail validation on submit.
permission_deniedForces an access-denied outcome on a sub-account or transfer submission.
confirm_dialogForces the native JS confirm() interstitial on the transfer form submit button.
session_timeoutForces the current request to be treated as having an expired session.
slowAdds an artificial delay before responding.
server_errorForces an unhandled HTTP 500 response.

Example: /member/M-10001?fault=slow or /member/M-10001/transfer?fault=confirm_dialog.
